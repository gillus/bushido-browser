Each round opens with a bow (REI 礼) — any input ends yours. Hitting an opponent
still bowing, or in the back, is DISHONOR 不名誉: your kill that round scores nothing.
A thrown weapon kills like a cut — miss, and you're unarmed until you walk back over it.
Whiffed a cut? Change stance and attack during its follow-through to chain into the new line.
Parry early in your own wind-up to feint — the cut is dropped, but you're open until back on guard.
